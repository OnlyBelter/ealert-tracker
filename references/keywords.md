# Research topic configuration

Edit `target_topics` in your private `config.json` to describe the research
areas that should appear in reports. The Skill uses these topics for semantic
relevance decisions; the collector does not discard candidates based on a
single literal keyword match.

The example configuration starts with:

- Bioinformatics and computational biology
- Multi-omics, genomics, and transcriptomics
- Single-cell and spatial biology
- Cancer biology, oncology, and tumor immunology
- Immunotherapy
- AI and machine learning for biology
- Proteomics, metabolomics, and microbiome research
- Systems biology

Keep each topic specific enough to distinguish direct relevance from a
coincidental mention. Remove or add topics to match your research focus.
